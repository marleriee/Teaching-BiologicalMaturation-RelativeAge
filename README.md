# Teaching-BiologicalMaturation-RelativeAge

**Group Work Material for Teaching on Biological Maturation and Relative Age in Sports**
**Date:** 11.05.2026 - 13:00 - 16:00

# Considering Biological Maturation in a Sports Team

## Setup

* Divide into 3 groups of 6 students.
* Use AI as a support tool, not as a complete solution.

## Scenario

You have been hired by a sports club to review their players and their performance. Each group will lead one of the three squads listed below.

| Group   | Squad to Analyse    | Sport Context        |
| ------- | ------------------- | -------------------- |
| Group 1 | U14 Girls Football  | Calendar-year cohort |
| Group 2 | U16 Boys Ice Hockey | Calendar-year cohort |
| Group 3 | U16 Boys Football   | Calendar-year cohort |

---

# Tasks

## 1. Selection Based on Performance Only

The club aims to participate in a regional championship, and only 18 of the 20 players can attend.

### a. Survival of the Fittest
Decide together which two players you would remove from the team if the selection were based only on performance.

Briefly explain your reasoning. You will return to these players later.

---

## 2. The Complaint

The club has now asked your group to analyse **relative age** and **biological maturation** within your squad.

### c. Assessing Biological Age

What methods could you use to assess players’ biological age?

Briefly:

* List the possible methods
* Discuss the strengths and limitations of each approach
* Decide which method(s) you will use based on the data available in the GitHub repository
* Provide a short justification for your choice

### d. Calculating Maturation and Relative Age

Calculate each player’s:

* Biological age / maturation status
* Relative age

### e. Visualising the Data

Prepare two figures showing the distribution of these markers across the team.

Your figures should be:

* Clear
* Self-explanatory
* Easy to interpret
* Suitable for presentation to the club chairman

### f. Grouping the Players

Divide the team into two or three groups based on your observations and analyses.

For example:

* Earlier-, average-, and later-maturing players
* Relative age quartiles
* Combined maturation and relative-age profiles

### g. Extreme Cases

Identify and describe three players who represent extreme cases in terms of biological age and/or relative age.

Explain:

* Why each player stands out
* How their profile may influence performance and development
* Any potential risks or advantages they may experience

---

## 3. Re-examining Your Two Cuts

Return to the two players you removed in Task 1.

Discuss:

* How do these players compare to the rest of the team in terms of biological maturation?
* How do they compare in terms of relative age?
* Would your original decision change now that you have this additional information?
* Explain and justify your reasoning.

---

## 4. Designing the Training Environment

Based on your findings, design a training camp and/or a weekly training structure that takes biological maturation and relative age into account.

Consider how you would organise training and development opportunities so that no player is disadvantaged because of:

* Birth date
* Growth pattern
* Maturation timing

You may consider:

* Grouping strategies
* Training intensity and load management
* Technical versus physical emphasis
* Player confidence and motivation
* Long-term athlete development

---

# Presentation

Each group should prepare a short PowerPoint presentation.

Imagine the other students are the board members of your sports club — convince them of your recommendations.

Your presentation should:

* Cover all four tasks
* Lead with your figures and analysis
* Present clear interpretations of your findings
* End with a practical recommendation for the club
